**Project Description:**
Syyncbiite is a web platform designed to bridge the gap between food surplus and food scarcity. It connects restaurants with excess food to NGOs and charities, ensuring that edible food reaches those in need instead of ending up in landfills.

**The Problem & Solution:**
*   **The Problem:** Every day, restaurants discard large amounts of edible food while millions go hungry. This lack of coordination leads to waste and lost opportunities to help the community.
*   **The Solution:** Syyncbiite provides a real-time platform where restaurants can instantly "sync" their surplus food availability. NGOs receive notifications and can claim the food for pickup, creating a seamless, life-saving logistics loop.

## Technical Details
**Technologies Used:**
*   **Frontend:** React (v19), Vite
*   **Styling:** CSS3 (Custom Variables, Flexbox/Grid)
*   **Routing:** React Router DOM (v7)
*   **State Management:** React Hooks
*   **Build Tool:** Vite

## Features
*   **Surplus Food Reporting:** Easy-to-use form for restaurants to list food details, quantity, expiry, and pickup locations.
*   **Impact Dashboard:** Visual tracking of total meals saved, people fed, and NGOs connected to show the real-world impact.
*   **Dual User Flow:** Dedicated interfaces for both Restaurants (Donors) and NGOs (Recipients).
*   **Responsive Design:** Accessible on various devices to ensure coordination happens on the go.
